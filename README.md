## A little about what I do

I'm a developer from Solapur, India, interested in building software that makes intelligent use of data, language models, and automation.

Most of my work revolves around **Python, AI systems, and backend engineering**. I enjoy going beyond simple LLM integrations — understanding how information is retrieved, how context is maintained, how agents use tools, and how the complete system behaves when deployed.

I like working at the intersection of:

- **AI Engineering:** Turning LLM capabilities into useful applications.
- **Retrieval Systems:** Making knowledge searchable, contextual, and accessible.
- **Backend Engineering:** Building APIs and services that support real applications.
- **Agentic AI:** Exploring reasoning loops, tool calling, memory, and workflow automation.

Currently pursuing my B.Tech in Information Technology at Walchand Institute of Technology, Solapur (2027).

Exploring ways to turn large collections of documents into useful, searchable knowledge systems.

### ⚙️ AI-powered Automation

Building Python-based automation systems that connect APIs, LLMs, and business workflows.
